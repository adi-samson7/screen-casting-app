Cast to another PC, a phone or a tablet that has no app: open a web page on it instead. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. Copy the recommended address (it's detected from your Wi-Fi) and open it in a browser on the other device.
  2. The page shows a 6-digit code.
  3. Come back to the TV App tab, enter that code and press Start casting.
If the page doesn't open, allow this app through the Windows firewall on private networks.
